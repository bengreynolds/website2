# Direction D - "Signal path". One reach becomes auditable data. Seven full-height `position: sticky` stages tell that chain in order and each names the projects that own it; a fixed index of the 7 stages and all 9 projects sits beside the story for its whole length, and a dense nine-project index below it means no project is reachable only through the narrative. Content lives in `src/siteData.js` (`pathStages`), spine and rail in `src/spa.css` sections 7 and 9, timeline re-bind in `src/rig-scrub.css`.
**01 The enclosure** - evidence: "Designed a 215-part enclosure carrying every module, cable route, and panel connector" (rig bullet); "a panel-built operant box with machined and PVC variants" (lick port bullet); "Sealed the enclosure with an O-ring groove, magnetic latch, and captive panel screws" (hypoxia bullet). Figure: the `buildup` 100-frame scroll scrub, opt-in because the sheet is 3 MB. Owners: training rig, lick port hardware, hypoxia chamber.
**02 Restraint and capture** - evidence: "Releasing and re-engaging the head clamp, 28 deg off the shoulder-screw axis ... solved as the four-bar they are" (tunnel caption); "Synchronized multi-camera capture with tracking and hardware control" (rig bullet); "NI-DAQ and PXI instrumentation added" on "x86_64 Ubuntu workstations" (reachAQ approach). Figure: `tunnel` demo. Owners: rig, reachAQ, deployment toolkit.
**03 Machine-learning tracking** - evidence: "DeepLabCut tracking ... into a closed loop" (rig approach); "Carried the trainer's core, video, device, inference, and behavior modules onto a new platform" (reachAQ bullet); "conversion of pose-estimation models between the analysis app and DeepLabCut formats" (toolkit bullet). No render exists because the stage is software, so the mono readout takes the figure column instead of a placeholder.
**04 Closed-loop control** - evidence: "Closed the loop on detected movement, with load-cell and presence sensing as guards" (rig bullet); "Six motor and CAN connectors, then the drive and sensing hardware" (pcb caption); "load-cell feedback verifying force, timing, and repeatability" (bench summary); "the board holds the trial state machine and the timing" (lickometer approach). Figures: `pcb` and `prosthetic-function` demos in one switcher.
**05 The delivery mechanism** - evidence: "barrier out, traverse, drop, scoop through 109 deg, lift, arm back to flush, barrier closed over the pellet, send" (pellet caption); "a four-stage gear train that indexes eight vials off a single servo" and "two shutter servos and two solenoid valves" (lickometer bullets); "nose cone, spout clamp, dropper, and electronics mount as one reward-port assembly" (lick port bullet). Figures: `pellet` pair and `lickrevolver-build`.
**06 Conversion and validation** - evidence: "every field where the two disagree is surfaced. Nine did."; "PyNWB writes the file and NWB Inspector validates it in the same pass"; "197,056 bytes"; "The validation report is written beside the NWB" (NWB Forge walkthrough steps, all read off one real captured run). Figure: that run's own `written` screenshot; the full six-step walkthrough stays on the case study rather than being mounted twice.
**07 Analysis** - evidence: "A headless analysis engine folded into ReachX" (summary); "Compared session summaries across mice, dates, rigs, and named condition groups" and "an intrasession mode for reach-level distributions" (bullets); "Nothing in the compute path imports a widget" (approach). Software again, so the readout takes the figure column. Owners: motion analysis suite, deployment toolkit.
**Palette** - one warm-neutral ramp, one amber accent, and the accent only ever marks something interactive (which is why the poster-scale stage numbers are `--text-subtle`, not amber). Dark is the signature: near-black page `#0e1013` with the spine deeper still at `#08090b`, amber `#ffb02e` at 10.7:1 on it. Light is a studio ground `#f6f4f1` with the amber darkened to `#984a00`, 5.14:1 on `--surface-sunken`, the darkest ground it has to clear. `--plate` stays light in both themes so the colour-accurate near-black CAD renders survive, which in dark mode makes them the only bright objects on the page.
**Type** - Syne Variable for the name and the stage titles at poster scale, Geist Variable for body copy, IBM Plex Mono for every stage number, label and readout. The families are named with their real `@fontsource` suffixes: the previous stylesheet asked for "Space Grotesk" while the package declares "Space Grotesk Variable", so every heading on the old site had been silently rendering in `system-ui`.
**Animation 1, `.rise`** - hierarchy: one staggered, time-based entrance orders the hero at first paint, and it is the only animated opacity on the page because it is the only animation guaranteed to finish.
**Animation 2, `.reveal`** - sequence: a 14px transform-only settle on `view()` tells the reader a new block below the fold has arrived; no opacity, because a scroll timeline that cannot advance holds its element at the start state and a faded keyframe would leave text permanently invisible.
**Animation 3, `.stage-body`** - sequence: the same settle, but bound to the stage track's own `view-timeline-name` rather than to `view()`, because a `view()` timeline freezes the instant its element is pinned and the copy would never resolve.
**Animation 4, the `buildup` scrub** - content, not decoration: scroll position is the frame index of a real assembly sequence, re-bound in `rig-scrub.css` to the track and ranged `cover 25%` to `cover 75%` so the 100 frames play across exactly the 200vh the stage is pinned for, at roughly 20px per frame.
**Animations 5 to 8** - the sticky pin (sequence: one stage holds the frame at a time, in order), wheel and arrow-key frame stepping (feedback: one notch is one frame), button-triggered sprite demos (state change: a mechanism runs because someone asked), and 140ms hover colour and border changes (feedback). That is the entire budget: no parallax, no infinite loops, no blur. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the pin, the scrub, the reveals and the load control all come off, and the seven stages resolve to plain hairline-separated sections in document order.
